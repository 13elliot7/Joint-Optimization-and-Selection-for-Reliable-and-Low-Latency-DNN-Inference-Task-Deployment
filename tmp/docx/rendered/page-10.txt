Reviewer#2, Concern#7: The paper only considers reliability and delay as
optimization objectives, but does not consider energy consumption or cost.

Author response:
Thanks for the suggestion. We have incorporated total energy consumption as an
optimization objective. The revised energy model includes computation energy and
transmission energy. DAREED uses energy utility in candidate scoring, mutation
guidance, local search, and final deployment selection. We did not include monetary
cost as a primary objective in this revision because cloud-edge-end pricing models
vary significantly across platforms, while energy consumption is directly measurable
and closely related to resource efficiency.

Author action:
We revised Section 4 to add the total energy consumption model and objective. We
revised Section 5 and Section 6 to include energy-aware algorithm mechanisms and
energy-related performance metrics.
