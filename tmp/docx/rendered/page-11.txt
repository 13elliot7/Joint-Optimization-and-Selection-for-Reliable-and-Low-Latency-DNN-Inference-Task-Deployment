Response to Reviewer #3

Reviewer#3, Concern#1: RDODA is essentially a direct application of classic NSGA-
II using standard genetic operations. It lacks customized algorithmic designs tailored
to DNN graph dependencies or edge networks.

Author response:
Thanks for the critical comment. We have redesigned the algorithm and renamed it
DAREED. The revised algorithm is no longer a direct use of standard NSGA-II.
DAREED incorporates DNN topology and dynamic resource states into the whole
evolutionary process. Customized algorithmic designs tailored to DNN graph
dependencies or edge networks, Specifically, it builds DAG context information,
initializes the population with topology-aware and resource-aware modes, performs
DAG block crossover, applies resource-aware skew mutation, repairs infeasible
individuals, uses constraint-dominance sorting, refines elite individuals through local
search, and selects the final deployment according to request preferences and energy
cost.

Author action:
We rewrote Section 5 as “Algorithm Design” for DAREED and added detailed
descriptions of each customized mechanism.

Reviewer#3, Concern#2: The strategy of assigning negative fitness values to
individuals violating the delay constraint is overly simplistic and may destroy
population diversity.

Author response:
Thanks for the suggestion. We replaced the previous penalty treatment with a
constraint-aware mechanism. DAREED evaluates constraint violation separately from
objective values. Feasible individuals dominate infeasible ones, and infeasible
individuals with smaller violations are retained when useful for guiding search. This
approach maintains population diversity while reducing the probability of selecting
severely infeasible solutions.

Author action:
We revised Section 5 to include constraint-aware repair and constraint-dominance
sorting.
