Response to Associate Editor

Associate Editor Comment: The manuscript requires major revision. The key
concerns include insufficient algorithmic novelty, unrealistic modeling assumptions,
unclear mathematical formulations, weak experimental evaluation, and writing quality
issues. The authors are also encouraged to consider additional optimization objectives
such as energy consumption and cost, and to test the framework under larger and
more dynamic network conditions.

Author response:
Thanks for the editor’s summary and guidance. We have conducted a major revision
to address these issues systematically.

First, the system model has been redesigned from a static node-reliability model to a
dynamic cloud-edge-end inference model. The revised model captures online DNN
request arrivals, running task blocks, time-varying node and link load, heat
accumulation, dynamic node reliability, dynamic link reliability, inference accuracy,
critical-path inference delay, and total energy consumption.

Second, the algorithm has been redesigned and renamed as DAREED. Rather than
directly applying a generic NSGA-II framework, DAREED embeds DNN DAG
information and dynamic resource states into initialization, crossover, mutation,
repair, constraint-dominance sorting, elite local search, and final preference-based
selection.

Third, the experimental design has been expanded. The revised performance analysis
includes state-of-the-art and stronger baselines, repeated random-seed experiments
with standard deviation reporting, Pareto-front quality metrics, scalability tests,
dynamic degradation tests, cloud-side stress tests, and ablation studies.

Author action:
We revised the Abstract, Introduction, Motivation, Problem Formulation, Algorithm
Design, Performance Analysis, Related Work, and Conclusion sections accordingly.
We also proofread the manuscript to improve terminology consistency and writing
quality.
