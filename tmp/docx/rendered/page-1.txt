Re: Response to reviewers

Dear Editor,

We thank you and the reviewers for the constructive comments and for the
opportunity to revise our manuscript. We have carefully studied all comments and
have substantially revised the manuscript in terms of system modeling, algorithm
design, experimental evaluation, related work, and writing quality.

In the revised manuscript, the deployment problem is reformulated for dynamic
cloud-edge-end DNN inference. The revised model considers DAG-structured
inference dependencies, time-varying node and link states, dynamic service reliability,
inference accuracy, inference delay, and total energy consumption. We also redesign
the algorithm as DAREED: DAG-Aware Reliable and Energy-Efficient
Evolutionary Deployment algorithm, which incorporates DAG-aware evolutionary
operators, resource-aware variation, constraint-aware selection, elite refinement, and
preference-based deployment selection. The performance analysis section has been
expanded with parameter sensitivity, Pareto quality, overall online deployment
performance, cloud-side stress tests, dynamic degradation tests, ablation studies,
scalability evaluation, and application preference scenarios.

The comments are reproduced below, followed by our point-by-point responses and
corresponding actions.
