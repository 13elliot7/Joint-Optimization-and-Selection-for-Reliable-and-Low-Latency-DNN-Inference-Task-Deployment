Author response:

Author action:
We revised Section 2 and updated the reference list. The revised Related Work
section now explicitly compares existing studies with the proposed DAREED design
from the perspectives of DNN topology, dynamic reliability, link reliability, energy
consumption, and preference-based multi-objective deployment.

Reviewer#2, Concern#2: The authors assume an oversimplistic constant service
reliability of computing nodes during execution, which is impractical in real systems.

Author response:
Thanks for the comment. We agree and have revised the model. Node service
reliability is no longer constant. It is dynamically updated according to node load and
heat state. This better reflects mobile edge environments where overloaded or
overheated nodes may become less stable during long-running inference service.

Author action:
We added dynamic service reliability and dynamic inference accuracy equations in
Section 4 and added dynamic degradation experiments in Section 6.

Reviewer#2, Concern#3: The authors only consider reliability of computing nodes
while ignoring the reliability of communication links.

Author response:
Thanks for this important suggestion. The revised model includes communication link
reliability. Link reliability changes with link load and heat state, and the service
reliability of a DNN request considers both the computing nodes and communication
links traversed by input uploading, intermediate dependency transmission, and output
returning.
